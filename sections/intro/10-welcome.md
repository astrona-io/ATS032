# Welcome To The Course

This course develops the official Cluster Hardening objectives for Certified Kubernetes Security Specialist (CKS). The certification uses a performance-based examination. The course is independently authored reading material and does not claim to simulate the examination.

Each section starts with its own introduction and learning outcomes. Each module provides goals, developed teaching and a final summary. Lesson counts follow the subject rather than a fixed number.

YAML knowledge checks are registered separately for future presentation. They follow the relevant summaries and include original questions and answer feedback.

Read the explanations and worked examples to identify the responsible component, intended operation and limits of each claim. The command output on the pages comes from real runs in the playgrounds and labs. Your names, addresses and ages can differ.

The expanded chapters open with a concrete problem and state measurable objectives and the required starting knowledge. Mechanism diagrams connect the responsible components, while worked examples explain the decisions and their limits. Summaries collect the conclusions after each complete topic.

Each active module has a private playground. Graded tasks follow the readings they practise and use separate environments. Complete the task before comparing your result with the solution. Host-level exercises target only disposable node containers or the private virtual machine of the lab.
